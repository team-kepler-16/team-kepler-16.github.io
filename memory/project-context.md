# Project Context

- **Goal:** Develop a Mars-only solution for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 challenge [Abandoned but Not Forgotten: Storytelling About NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/abandoned-but-not-forgotten-storytelling-about-nasas-discarded-equipment-on-the-moon-and-mars/).
- **Current stack:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and jQuery 4.0.0 loaded from the official CDN. Additional technologies may be introduced later.
- **JavaScript preference:** Use jQuery for DOM work when convenient; use native JavaScript when clearer.
- **Code organization:** Keep `js/mars.js` as the Three.js scene entry point, with globe interaction in `js/mars/globe-controls.js` and marker rendering in `js/mars/marker-field.js`. Keep shared site controls and translations in `js/script.js`.
- **Responsive requirement:** The site must display and remain usable on both desktop and mobile devices.
- **Mars visualization:** Show a slowly auto-rotating 3D Mars sphere with cartoon shading, starting slightly zoomed out, on a full-viewport canvas behind the floating navbar. Frame the globe in the available area below the navbar; zoom may extend beyond the screen edges. Randomly place interactive cyan discs on the globe surface that link to Google, with surface-conforming soft halos, enlarged click targets, paired radar-style waves, and a red outline, interior, and halo on hover that pauses automatic rotation. Users can rotate it by clicking and dragging horizontally with the mouse or dragging with one finger, around its vertical axis, and zoom with the mouse wheel or a two-finger pinch. It uses Three.js and the NASA/JPL/Caltech cartoon texture in `assets/mars-texture-cartoon.png`, credited to [NASA's Mars 3D resources](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars/).
- **Homepage title:** “NASA: 50 Years of Landings on Mars,” translated for the Spanish locale and aligned left of the navbar on desktop. On small screens, center it on one line above the centered navigation controls.
- **Credits:** Keep the Mars texture attribution and source link on `about.html`.
- **Design and navigation:** Use a modern Mars-exploration visual language with layered space backgrounds, mineral/rust tones, editorial typography, atmospheric lighting, and cyan signal accents that match the interactive markers. Show the 18 point labels in a right-side list, localized with the rest of the interface. Keep the floating navigation controls for sound mute/unmute, English/Spanish locale, and About. Ensure the design remains readable and usable on desktop and mobile.
- **Documentation language:** English.
